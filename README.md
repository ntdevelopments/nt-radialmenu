## Preview
[![Preview video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/4JTrRKfpoSY/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4JTrRKfpoSY)

## Features
- Citizen, Clothing, Emotes, Job and Blips submenus
- Job actions: duty, inspect, tow, flip, repair, clean
- 4 models, 6 presets: Classic, Minimal, Neon, Gold, Crimson, Compact
- Custom colours, drag to move, size, click sound
- Languages: English, Turkish, French
